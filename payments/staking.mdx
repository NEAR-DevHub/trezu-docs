Trezu allows you to stake your treasury's tokens through third-party services, so you can earn rewards while keeping your funds secure.

<Info>
  Confidential treasuries cannot stake their tokens, as staking is an inherently public operation
</Info>

***

## Staking

Navigate to the **`Earn`** section in the left sidebar, you will see a list of third-party services, select the one you want to use to stake your tokens.

<Columns cols={2}>

  <Card title="NEAR Token">
    You can stake your NEAR tokens through the [NEAR Staking portal](https://near-staking.trezu.org/) by Trezu
  </Card>
  <Card title="USDC/USDT">
    You can earn yield on your USDC and USDT tokens through [Rhea Finance](https://x.rhea.finance/en/earn)
  </Card>
</Columns>

Connect your treasury to the service by selecting the Trezu Wallet option, you will be asked with which of your treasuries you want to connect.

<Columns cols={2}>

  <img src="/assets/docs/staking/trezu-wallet.jpeg" alt="Trezu Wallet" />
  <img src="/assets/docs/staking/trezu-wallet-treasury.png" alt="Trezu Wallet Treasury" />

</Columns>

Once connected, you can simply interact with the service to stake your tokens.

The Trezu Wallet will automatically translate your actions into proposals for your team to vote on the staking operation.

<img src="/assets/docs/staking/trezu-sign.jpeg" alt="Trezu Wallet" />

The proposals will be listed in the **`Proposals`** section as `Function Call`,  and team members with the [Finance](/governance/members-and-roles#finance) role will be able to review and vote on them.  

<img src="/assets/docs/staking/proposals.jpeg" alt="Trezu Wallet" />

Once approved, the staking operation will be executed automatically by the system, and your tokens will be staked.

---

## Unstaking & Withdrawal

When you want to unstake your tokens, you will need to follow the same process as staking, but this time you will be interacting with the service to unstake your tokens.

As with the staking operation, the unstaking and withdrawal operations will be listed as proposals in the **`Proposals`** section, and team members with the [Finance](/governance/members-and-roles#finance) role will be able to review and vote on them.

<Tip>
  Remember that, depending on the token you are unstaking, there may be a waiting period before you can withdraw your tokens. For example, NEAR tokens have a 24-hour unstaking period.
</Tip>
